VR product design · Team lead

conVRsation

Rehearsing the first conversation in a crisis
I interviewed police officers and counselors about gaps in psychological first aid training. My team and I used their accounts to design a VR prototype in which trainees could practice the conversation more than once.

Period
March – July 2026
My role
Led the team and field interviews, co-designed the training and helped build the prototype
Tools & methods
Psychological first aid (PFA), VR scenario design, prototyping

What practitioners wanted to practice

I designed and conducted interviews with police officers and counselors. They already learned psychological first aid through lectures and role-play. They wanted more chances to encounter different reactions, reflect on their own responses and try the same situation again.

From observation to practical support

We structured the scenario around three steps in psychological first aid. Trainees observe how the person is doing (Look), listen to their concerns (Listen) and connect them with support (Link). I worked with teammates and mentors to choose which scenes to demonstrate and in what order to build them. I also helped write the dialogue and develop the prototype.

Demonstrating the prototype

I presented the project and gave a demo in English during the Silicon Valley round of KAIST’s Global Entrepreneurship Summer School. We used the prototype to show how practitioners’ feedback shaped the scenario and gathered responses to the design. Institutional adoption and training effectiveness remain open questions.
