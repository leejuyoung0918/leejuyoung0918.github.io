Reading Practice Web App

Designing the path from sign-in to reading practice
The app was designed for people who wanted to practice reading speed and comprehension. I designed and built the path from sign-in to the home screen so readers could find their next step and focus on the text.

Date
Winter 2023
Role
Interface design and frontend development
Tools
Figma, React, React Router, Firebase Authentication

What should the reader see first?

Reading a menu or a set of instructions also takes effort. In Figma, I worked out the order in which information should appear on the sign-in and home screens. The design gave priority to what readers needed to look at first and what action they could take next.

Connecting sign-in to the app

I built the sign-in and home pages in React and used React Router for navigation. I connected the form to Firebase Authentication, managed the signed-in state and handled authentication errors.

What I learned from the screen transitions

Designing sign-in and the home screen together made me consider how much a reader needed to read and do just to navigate between them. In an app for reading practice, every instruction and transition also contributes to the reader’s workload.
